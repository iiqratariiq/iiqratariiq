### About me

I work across two fields that rarely sit side by side: **speech therapy** and **technical support**. In therapy, I work out how a word gets formed or how a device can match the way someone thinks. In support, I read logs to trace what a system actually did. The method is the same: follow the signal, rule out what it isn't, and explain it clearly.

I'm here to start building where those meet: **software for neuroscience, speech, and language**.

### Right now

- 🗣️ Running [BOL Speech and Language Therapy Clinic](https://www.bolspeechclinic.com/meet-your-therapist), including its website, scheduling, and custom teletherapy tools
- 🛠️ Tracing telematics logs for ELD compliance as a Technical Support Specialist at Motive
